System Functionalities

A

QR Code Ticket Generation & Validation

Instant digital ticket creation with unique QR codes for secure entrance validation. Tickets are generated immediately upon purchase and can be scanned at park gates for contactless entry.

B

Mobile Payment Processing

Secure online payment system supporting multiple payment methods with automatic price calculation based on ticket type (Day Pass: R350, Standard: R250, VIP: R650).

C

Real-Time Queue Management

Live attraction wait time tracking and visitor flow monitoring. Users can view current queue status for all attractions and plan their park visit efficiently.

D

Admin Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive staff interface with real-time visitor counts, revenue tracking, user management capabilities, and operational insights for data-driven decision making.
